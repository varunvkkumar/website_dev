MineOpt

MineOpt is a unique tool for optimizing mining projects to maximize value. It includes the following modules:

  • PitOpt: Determining nested pit shells based on value.
  • AutoDesign: Designing pits from pit shells.
  • SchedOpt: Schedule Optimizer including dumping and hauling.
  • Stochastic Optimisation: Stochastic analysis of strategic mine plans to determine ‘Hill of Value’.

Stage 1: PitOpt

PitOpt helps in finding the best value in your deposit:

  • Determine the Optimum pits for a deposit for given cost, sale prices, and geotechnical slope consideration.
  • Determine Nested pit shells to understand best phasing considerations for mine plans.
  • Visualize pit progression based on value ($).
  • Report and view pit-by-pit graphs.
  • Applicable for stratified geological models or block models for base metals or bulk commodities.

Stage 2: AutoDesign

AutoDesign allows you to easily design and create reserves:

  • Automatic parametric design from pit shells.
  • Try different design options for varying bench height, berm width, and slope combinations.
  • Create mining solids to represent Smallest Mining Units (SMU).
  • Create and report ROM reserves for use in SchedOpt for scheduling.

Stage 3: SchedOpt

SchedOpt helps in NPV maximization with:

  • Optimum Mining Sequence for a schedule.
  • Optimum dumping sequence for in-pit and external dumps.
  • In-pit dumps consider lag with the working face.
  • Considers building haulage network of shortest paths and selection of optimal haul roads for each block by period.
  • 3D display/schedule animation and export of end of period surfaces to DXF and STL formats.
